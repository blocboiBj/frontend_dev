Our Story

Founded in 2015, Vickie's Kitchen brings the rich, bold flavors of Liberian tradition to your plate. Every dish tells a story of heritage, family recipes passed down through generations, and a passion for wholesome, hearty meals.

From our famous palm butter soup to crispy cassava bread, we serve comfort food that feels like home. Our commitment to quality and authenticity has made us a beloved spot for food lovers seeking a taste of Liberia. Join us on this culinary journey and experience the warmth and flavor of Vickie's Kitchen.
